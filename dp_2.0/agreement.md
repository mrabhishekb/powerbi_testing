# Data Product Agreement

This Data Product is provided for internal analytics and dashboarding purposes.

## Permitted Use

- Use for business intelligence, trend analysis, and reporting.
- Use derived insights in internal presentations and planning.

## Prohibited Use

- Do not expose raw data externally without authorization.
- Do not use this Data Product for regulatory or legal reporting without independent validation.

## Data Handling

- Treat all data as internal.
- Follow organization security and access-control policies.

## Retention

- Consumers are responsible for handling extracts according to organizational retention policies.
